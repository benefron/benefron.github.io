### Design Principles Applied

1. **Visual Hierarchy** - Clear distinction between primary and secondary content
2. **White Space** - Proper spacing for readability and focus
3. **Color Psychology** - Blue for trust, purple for creativity
4. **Consistency** - Unified design language across all pages
5. **Accessibility** - Good contrast ratios, readable fonts
6. **Interactivity** - Hover effects, smooth transitions
7. **Professional Appeal** - Clean, modern, industry-standard design
