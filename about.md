# About Me

<div class="info-card">
  <h3>🎓 Current Position</h3>
  <p><strong>Postdoctoral Fellow</strong> in the Brain Inspired Computing Lab<br>
  Electrical Engineering Department, University of Liège, Belgium</p>
</div>

I am a neuroscientist passionate about bridging the gap between **biological intelligence** and **artificial systems**. My research focuses on applying principles from neuroscience and biology to advance technologies in machine learning, edge computing, sensor integration, and communication.

With a PhD in Systems Neuroscience from the **Weizmann Institute of Science**, I develop algorithms inspired by how neurons process information. My doctoral research focused on multi-modal information integration in the mouse auditory and somatosensory cortices during active sensing, using state-of-the-art methods including automated behavior tracking, machine learning, and advanced modeling.
