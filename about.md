## About Me

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Brain Inspired Computing Lab at the Electrical Engineering Department, University of Liège, Belgium. My research focuses on applying principles from neuroscience and biology to advance technologies in machine learning, edge computing, sensor integration, and communication.

With a PhD in Systems Neuroscience from the Weizmann Institute of Science, I bridge the gap between biological neural systems and computational models, developing algorithms inspired by how neurons process information. My doctoral research focused on multi-modal information integration in the mouse auditory and somatosensory cortices during active sensing, using state-of-the-art methods including automated behavior tracking, machine learning, and advanced modeling.

My goal is to contribute to the tech industry as a researcher, developing innovative projects that leverage biological insights for technological breakthroughs. I'm particularly interested in positions where I can apply my expertise in neural computation, data analysis, and experimental design to create cutting-edge solutions.

## Research Interests
