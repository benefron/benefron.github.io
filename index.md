## About My Research

I am a **Postdoctoral Fellow** in the Brain Inspired Computing Lab at the Electrical Engineering Department, **University of Liège**. With a PhD in Systems Neuroscience from the **Weizmann Institute of Science**, I focus on implementing neuroscience and biological insights into cutting-edge technologies.

My research bridges the gap between biological neural systems and computational models, developing algorithms inspired by how neurons process information. I work on projects related to machine learning algorithms, edge computing, sensor integration, and communication, applying principles from the nervous system and neuronal computation.
