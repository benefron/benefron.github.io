## Current Focus

**Postdoctoral Researcher** at **University of Liège** | Brain-Inspired Computing Lab

I develop closed-loop control systems and data analysis pipelines for neuronal cultures on high-density microelectrode arrays (HDMEA). My work combines **theoretical neuroscience**, **control theory**, **neuroengineering**, and **bio-inspired machine learning** to understand and manipulate neuronal dynamics.
