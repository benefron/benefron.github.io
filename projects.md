## PhD Research: Multi-Modal Sensory Integration

### Neural Encoding of Self-Generated Sounds During Active Sensing

**Weizmann Institute of Science (2017-2024)**

My doctoral research investigated how the brain processes sounds that animals generate themselves during exploration. Key achievements:

- Designed and engineered complex behavioral experiments combining neurophysiology with automated tracking
- Analyzed neuronal and behavioral data using machine learning and advanced statistical modeling (GLM)
- Discovered novel mechanisms of auditory-somatosensory integration during active whisking
- Published findings in *Current Biology* (2025)

**Technical Skills Demonstrated:**
- Python & MATLAB programming for data analysis
- Hardware engineering (Arduino, custom behavioral setups)
- Machine learning & statistical modeling
- Automated behavior tracking and analysis
- Neural signal processing
