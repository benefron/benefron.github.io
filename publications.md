## Research Impact

My research focuses on understanding how the brain integrates information from multiple sensory modalities during active behavior. This work has implications for:

- **Neural Computation:** Understanding principles of multi-modal integration
- **Bio-Inspired AI:** Developing algorithms based on biological sensory processing
- **Robotics:** Designing active sensing systems for autonomous agents
- **Neuroscience:** Advancing our understanding of cortical processing
