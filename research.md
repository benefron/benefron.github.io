## Active Research Projects

### HDMEA Neuroengineering Platform Development
**Collaboration with imec Life Sciences Technology**

Extending the capabilities of high-density microelectrode arrays (HDMEA) for advanced in vitro neuroscience:

- **Real-time data processing**: Online spike sorting and network activity analysis
- **Closed-loop control systems**: Designing feedback experiments to direct neuronal dynamics
- **Advanced analytics**: Dimensionality reduction, latent manifold analysis, connectivity mapping
- **Protocol automation**: Streamlining experimental workflows for reproducibility

**Role**: Technical liaison between imec engineering team and neuroscience applications


### Neuronal Culture Dynamics & Learning
**Investigating adaptive behavior in cultured networks**

- **Plasticity mechanisms**: Exploring how neuronal cultures learn and adapt under controlled stimulation
- **Network connectivity**: Mapping functional and structural relationships in developing cultures
- **Latent dynamics**: Uncovering low-dimensional manifolds underlying high-dimensional neural activity
- **Computational modeling**: Building predictive models of network behavior


### Clinical Neuroscience Applications
**Partnership with University Hospital Liège & GIGA Institute**

Applying HDMEA technology to understand pathological neural activity:

- **Neuronal-glial interactions**: Characterizing network dysfunction in disease models
- **Biomarker discovery**: Identifying electrophysiological signatures of pathology
- **Translational potential**: Bridging in vitro findings to clinical contexts

## PhD Research (2017-2024)

### Multi-Modal Sensory Integration in Active Sensing
**Weizmann Institute of Science** | Lampl Lab

Investigated how the mouse brain integrates self-generated sounds with tactile information during active whisking—a fundamental question in sensory neuroscience.

**Key Achievements:**
- Discovered mice can hear and neurally encode whisker-generated sounds
- Demonstrated behavioral relevance of auditory-somatosensory integration
- Developed automated behavior tracking and machine learning analysis pipelines
- Published findings in **Current Biology** with widespread media coverage

**Technical Innovation:**
- Custom Arduino-based experimental control systems
- Real-time behavioral tracking using computer vision
- Advanced GLM modeling of neural responses
- Integration of electrophysiology with automated behavior
